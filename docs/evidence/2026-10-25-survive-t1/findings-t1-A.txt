[F7] LOW (reasoned, not reproduced) - Stream landing is not atomic across tables.
  stream_raw, stream_events and stream_checkpoint are separate jobs in one dlt run. Dedup trusts stream_raw, and the
  high-water mark is restored from stream_checkpoint. After a crash part-way through a load:
    - if stream_raw landed but stream_events did not, those edits are in `seen` and are never landed again. The edits
      are lost for good.
    - if stream_raw landed but the checkpoint did not, the restored high_water is lower than the real maximum, so later
      late flags can differ from the oracle (which recomputes from stream_raw).
    - dlt also retries pending packages from out/dlt-stream on the next run, after StreamConsumer.__init__ has already
      read `seen`, so a resumed package and the re-received events could both land, giving duplicate event_ids.
  Cheap fix: derive high_water at start-up as max(timestamp) over stream_raw (by the documented rule it is the same
  value), and derive `seen` after finishing any pending dlt package (pipeline.load() before reading the lake).
