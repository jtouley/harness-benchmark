/cadence:cadence Add monthly category budgets to ledgerlite. `budget set CATEGORY LIMIT` sets a monthly spending limit across all accounts (LIMIT must be positive, else exit 2); `budget list` prints `CATEGORY: LIMIT` sorted by category. `report budget YYYY-MM` prints one line per budgeted category: `CATEGORY: spent SPENT of LIMIT (PCT%)`, with ` OVER` appended when spent > limit. Spending is the sum of the negative amounts in that category and month, as a positive number (refunds do not reduce it); PCT is spent/limit*100 rounded to the nearest integer; money is shown with 2 decimals. When `tx add` or `import` pushes a category's spending for that month past its limit, the command also prints `alert: CATEGORY over budget for YYYY-MM (SPENT/LIMIT)`, once per category per command. SPENT in the alert is the month's spending after the whole command. Budgets persist in the store; existing stores without budgets keep working.
