Ownership of the late split is also unclear: the engine, or the dbt incremental models (see G-8).

S6. Check 6 on en.wikipedia.org. The stream carries `server_name` (en.wikipedia.org) and `wiki` (enwiki). The spec uses "en.wikipedia.org" and "wiki id" without tying them. The mapping through sitematrix is implied, not stated. Tie-breaking for equal window counts is not stated. Both are non-blocking.

S8. Invalid pipelines/*.yml. The spec requires a schema check that reports file, line and problem. The schema is not specified, and there are no exit tests for the engine itself. Non-blocking.
