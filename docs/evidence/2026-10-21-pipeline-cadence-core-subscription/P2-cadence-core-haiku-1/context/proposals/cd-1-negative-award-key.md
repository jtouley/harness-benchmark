---
decision_id: cd-1-negative-award-key
status: proposed
question: What is the unit for counting awards with a net negative obligation in check 3?
choice: The award is the prime award unique key. An award is negative when the sum of its FY2024 federal_action_obligation is below zero.
rationale: The check asks about awards, and one award has many transactions (modifications and de-obligations), so the net per award is the measure. The option rejected is counting single transactions with a negative amount, which counts reversals as awards.
